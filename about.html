What we do

One line from reactor to container.

Manufacture

We manufacture speciality and industrial chemistry to specification — from batch reactor to finished lot, tested before release.

Trade & source

For non-manufactured grades we source directly from vetted plants — no marketplace margin, no middleman risk.

Test in-house

Our QC laboratory verifies assay, moisture and packaging standards on every batch before loading.

Document for export

COA, MSDS, HS classification and complete EXIM paperwork — handled as part of the order, not an extra.

How we work

Three steps, zero surprises.

01

Specify

You email product, grade, quantity and destination port. We reply with price, lead time and COA sample within 24 working hours.

02

Produce + test

The batch is manufactured or sourced, tested in the QC lab, and released with its certificate of analysis.

03

Ship

Documented loading, retained samples archived, full EXIM paperwork — on FOB, CIF or EXW terms.
