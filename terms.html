Terms & conditions

The rules of engagement for buying from Vanguard Chemical Solutions LLP.

Home›Terms & Conditions

1. Quotations & orders

Quotations are valid 15 days unless stated. An order is binding only on written order confirmation. Prices exclude taxes, duties and freight unless agreed. Incoterms 2020 (FOB / CIF / EXW) apply.

2. Quality & inspection

Each batch is tested and supplied with COA + MSDS. Third-party inspection (SGS / Intertek) is welcome before loading at buyer's cost. Claims must be raised within 10 days of receipt.

3. Packing & marking

Standard export packing as quoted — bags, drums, IBC or jumbo. Marine-safe marking and fumigation certificates provided where required.

4. Documentation

Invoice, packing list, COA, MSDS and certificate of origin are provided with each shipment. Other documents on request.

5. Liability

Liability is limited to the invoice value of the affected batch. Neither party is liable for events beyond reasonable control.

6. Compliance & law

Buyer and seller comply with all export-control and chemical regulations. Terms are governed by Indian law; courts at Mumbai have exclusive jurisdiction.
