The rules of engagement for buying from, and browsing, Vanguard Chemical Solutions LLP.

1. Quotations, Orders and Contract

Quotations are valid for 15 days unless stated otherwise and are subject to product availability. An order becomes binding only upon the written order confirmation issued by the Company. Prices are quoted in the currency stated and, unless agreed otherwise, are exclusive of taxes, duties and freight. Incoterms 2020 (FOB / CIF / EXW or as agreed) apply to export shipments.

2. Product Specifications and Quality

Products are supplied against the agreed written specification and are accompanied by a batch Certificate of Analysis (COA) and Safety Data Sheet (SDS/MSDS) on request. Manufacturing tolerances, standard industry variations and country-specific regulatory limits apply. Third-party inspection (SGS, Intertek or mutually agreed agency) may be arranged at buyer cost upon written request before dispatch.

3. Product Use and Safety

Buyers are responsible for determining the suitability of any product for their intended application and for compliance with local handling, storage, transport, occupational-health and environmental regulations. The Company supplies chemicals for industrial and professional use only; products must not be repackaged for retail or resold to consumers without appropriate labelling and regulatory compliance. Safety Data Sheets must be read and understood before use.

4. Payment and Delivery

Payment terms are as stated on the order confirmation (advance, LC, or as agreed). Title passes upon receipt of full payment; risk passes as per the applicable Incoterm. Delivery periods are estimates; delays due to logistics, customs or force majeure do not constitute breach. The buyer is responsible for import licences, permits and duties at destination.

5. Claims and Returns

Claims for quantity or visible damage must be notified within 7 days of receipt; quality claims must be supported by the batch number, COA reference and an independent test report, and notified within 30 days. Products correctly supplied to specification are not returnable. The Company liability, in any event, is limited to the invoice value of the affected batch.

6. Website Content and Intellectual Property

All content on this website - text, specifications, logo, layout and data - is the property of Vanguard Chemical Solutions LLP and is protected by applicable intellectual-property law. Information is provided in good faith for professional buyers; the Company does not warrant that the website content is error-free or fit for any particular purpose, and product ranges, grades and specifications may change without notice.

7. Export and Regulatory Compliance

Buyer and seller shall comply with all applicable export-control, customs, chemical-regulatory (including REACH, GHS and local chemical rules), sanctions and anti-bribery laws. The Company may refuse any order that would violate applicable law.

8. Force Majeure and Governing Law

Neither party is liable for failure caused by events beyond reasonable control, including natural events, strikes, port closures, regulatory action or supply failure.
