Privacy policy

How this website and your enquiry emails are handled. No tracking, no forms, no nonsense.

Home›Privacy Policy

1. Data we collect

We operate no forms, accounts or analytics. When you email us, we receive your name, company details and message content. Hosting access logs (IP, browser, time) are kept for security only.

2. How data is used

Enquiry data is used solely to respond to you and manage the business relationship. It is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

3. Cookies

We set no advertising cookies. Third-party fonts may apply their own technical processes under their policies.

4. Retention

Correspondence is kept as long as required for the business relationship and legal record-keeping, then securely deleted.

5. Your rights

Request access, correction or deletion by emailing sales@vanguardchemicals.in. We respond within 30 days.
