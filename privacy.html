Privacy policy

How this website — and your enquiry emails — are handled. No tracking. No forms. No nonsense.

Home›Privacy Policy

1. Data We Collect

We do not operate forms, accounts or analytics on this website. When you email us, we receive your name, company details, email address and the content of your message. Server access logs (IP address, browser type, date/time) are maintained by the hosting provider for security and uptime only.

2. How We Use Data

Enquiry emails are used solely to respond to your request, prepare quotations and maintain the commercial relationship. We never sell, rent or trade your contact details. Your data is shared only with logistics partners, inspection agencies or freight forwarders as needed to execute your order.

3. Cookies

This website does not set tracking or advertising cookies. If local fonts or scripts are served by third parties (e.g., Google Fonts), their standard technical processes may apply under their own policies.

4. Retention

Enquiry correspondence is retained for as long as necessary for the business relationship and applicable tax, customs and legal record-keeping requirements, after which it is securely deleted.

5. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures - TLS/HTTPS on the live site, access-controlled mailboxes and endpoint protection - to protect correspondence against loss or unauthorised access. No method of transmission is 100% secure; please avoid sending confidential formulation data by unencrypted email unless agreed.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or withdraw consent for further contact, by emailing sales@vanguardchemicals.in. We respond within 30 days.

7. Contact

Data protection queries: Vanguard Chemical Solutions LLP, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India - sales@vanguardchemicals.in.
